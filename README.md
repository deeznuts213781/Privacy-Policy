Privacy Policy — Nitroze Network Bot

Last updated: November 21, 2026

The Nitroze Network bot, is a ticket and carry-service management bot used exclusively on The Nitroze Network's Discord server. This page explains what information the bot collects, why, and how it's handled.

What we collect

The bot stores the following in a private database on our own hosting server. We do not collect anything beyond what's listed here.

From tickets you open:

Your Discord user ID and the ticket's channel ID, type, and status (open/claimed/closed)
The information you submit in a ticket's form — your Minecraft username (IGN), amount requested, in-game score or combat level, or a free-text description (for support tickets)
Timestamps: when the ticket was created, when you first sent a message in it, and when it was closed or logged

From completed carries:

The carrier's Discord user ID, the customer's Discord user ID, the carry type and amount, and the XP it earned
These records never expire and form the basis of the carrier leaderboard and level system

From reviews:

If you're DMed a review request after a carry, your 1–5 star rating and any comment you choose to leave, linked to the carrier you rated
You are never required to leave a review; declining to respond stores nothing

From Question of the Week submissions:

The question text and your Discord user ID, only after a staff member approves it. A rejected submission is never stored — it exists only as a Discord message until a moderator decides

From staff actions:

Which Discord roles a staff member has assigned to you via the bot's commands, and who assigned them
Nothing else about you is recorded unless it's a direct result of a command used on your ticket or account

What we do not store:
Message content, outside of two narrow cases: (1) a ticket's conversation is bundled into a transcript file when the ticket is closed, which is uploaded directly to a private Discord channel and is never saved to our own database or servers; (2) we detect that you've sent a first message in a new support ticket (to cancel an auto-close timer) but do not store what you said
Message content from any channel other than your own ticket
Any data unrelated to the ticket/carry system
Anything for members who have never opened a ticket, submitted a QOTW question, or been assigned a carrier role
Third-party services

When a Minecraft username is looked up (via /userinfo, or automatically on a role-request ticket), the bot queries the public Mojang API and the Hypixel public API to retrieve the account's UUID and any Discord account linked on Hypixel. These are public lookup services; we don't send them anything beyond the username you provide, and we don't control their own data practices.

How long data is kept

Ticket and carry records are kept indefinitely, since they're the basis of the server's leaderboard and carrier history. If you'd like your data removed, contact a server admin (see below) and we'll delete what we can without breaking another member's own records (for example, a review you left stays linked to the carrier's rating average, but we can remove your Discord ID from it on request).

Who can see this data

Only the bot and the server's staff team, through the bot's own commands (like /carrier-info), can access this information. It is never sold, shared with advertisers, or given to anyone outside the server's staff.

Changes to this policy

If what the bot collects changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top revised.

Contact

Questions about your data, or requests to have it removed, can be sent to @nejmiq on discord.
